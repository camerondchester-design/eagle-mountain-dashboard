What the city is talking about

Data centers

Meta operating and expanding; QTS building a 5-building campus; Tract and Google hold land. Oct 20: Corrigan Farms rezoned for a data center; a power plant would need a separate application.

civicrollcall.com

Budget and property taxes

Aug 18: 0.000900 rate (certified 0.000530) and $363,992,973 budget adopted 4-1; 28 public speakers. Property tax now dedicated to public safety.

utahledger.com

Roads and traffic

Lake Mountain Corridor alignment, Pony Express Pkwy to 7 lanes, Silver Lake school connections; funding is the hurdle.

Jul 30 joint session

Water and sewer

Plant expansion broke ground Oct 23. 5-year water costs $109.1M, wastewater $259M.

eaglemountain.gov

Commercial growth and zoning

Eagle's Landing (Smith's, Lowe's); Gateway Park 600-acre plan; Cedar Pass Ranch zone; alternative energy overlay revisions.

Mar 25 meeting
